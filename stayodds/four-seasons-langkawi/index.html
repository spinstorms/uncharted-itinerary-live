How is this StayOdds % calculated? ▾
35% Published Critics (81%) + 65% Verified Guests (9.4/10 → 94%) = 89% StayOdds
  • One headline percentage, zero double-counting: StayOdds % is simply the weighted average of whichever independent numeric scores exist above — never inflated or tweaked by hidden bonuses.
  • My Stay Score (/25): Our personal 5-category score (Value, Room Quality, Vibe, Food, Service — each out of 5, multiplied by 4 to convert to a 100% scale).
  • Published Critics & Video Reviewers (/100): Average of reviewers (including Walker Hotel Index by Ryan Walker on a 50-point scale normalized × 2, Nonstop Dan & Nonstop Dan Vlogs on a 100-point scale, The Flip-Flop Traveller on a 100-point scale, The Luxury Travel Expert on a 10-point scale, and Fat Voyage on a 20-point scale) only where an explicit numeric score is verified from a real source. When a reviewer is known to award a score/points total that cannot be verified from text or captions, it is marked “Score/points awarded — unable to verify exact figure” and excluded from numeric math.
  • Google Guests (/5★): Live guest rating from Google (stars × 20 = 100% scale).
  • Weighting rules: 40% My Stay / 30% Critics / 30% Guests when all three exist • 55% My Stay / 45% other source when two exist • 35% Critics / 65% Guests when we haven’t stayed yet • 100% when only one numeric source exists.
