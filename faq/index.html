How StayOdds. Works & Scores Hotels

Transparent math, verified sources, and zero made-up scores across stayodds.com.

How is the StayOdds % calculated?

StayOdds % is a clean weighted average of whichever verified numeric scores exist for a hotel — with zero hidden tweaks, no bonus points, and no double-counting:

40% My Stay · 30% Critics · 30% Guests 55% My Stay · 45% Guests 35% Critics · 65% Guests 100% Guest Rating

On every hotel breakdown page, you can expand “How is this StayOdds % calculated?” to see the exact numbers and weights that produced the final percentage.

What is the difference between First-Hand Verified and Global Directory?

First-Hand Verified hotels are properties we have personally stayed at, filmed, and rated across our 5-category /25 scorecard (plus shorter on-property tours we've filmed).

Global Directory hotels are iconic luxury resorts around the world that we haven't reviewed yet — we gather published critic scores, official awards, creator video tours, and live guest ratings so you can still compare them fairly side-by-side.

Why don’t MICHELIN Keys, Forbes stars, or YouTube reviews show a number?

Because we refuse to invent numbers. Official awards like MICHELIN Keys, Forbes Five-Star, and The World’s 50 Best Hotels are shown as verified badges linking directly to the awarding body.

Similarly, written reviews without a numeric rating (like The Points Guy) and independent YouTube hotel tours are linked directly as commentary so you can read or watch them yourself without us guessing a score.

What does the “Opinion Gap” mean?

The Opinion Gap measures how far apart our stay (or published critics) sit compared to real everyday guest reviews:

  • Strong Agreement (0–6 pts): Critics and real guests largely agree on what to expect.
  • Mostly Aligned (7–12 pts): Broadly consistent with a few trade-offs worth checking.
  • Opinions Differ (13+ pts): A noticeable gap — often where hype, service inconsistency, or value divides opinion.
Where do the live guest ratings come from?

Where licensed verified guest data is available, it is baked into the scorecard. Otherwise, each hotel page fetches the current live Google rating and review count on the fly and automatically recalculates the StayOdds % so scores stay fresh as properties renovate or change management.

Can I request a resort or report an error on a hotel page?

Absolutely! Use the modal anywhere on the site to suggest a hotel you’re comparing, or send us a quick correction via our Contact page.

Do booking links affect StayOdds scores?

Never. Some “Check Rates” buttons use affiliate links that may earn us a small commission at no extra cost to you if you book. They have zero influence on rankings, StayOdds %, or whether a property is included.

How do I compare two resorts head-to-head?

Head to the StayOdds. Directory and pick any two properties in the Head-to-Head Compare bar at the top, or click Compare VS from any individual hotel page or the World Hotel Map to line them up side-by-side.

Do you pay for your own hotel stays and flights?

Yes. We fund the vast majority of our stays and flights ourselves using cash or travel points we have personally earned. That’s why we can be completely candid about room wear, overpriced dining, or service slip-ups.

If a stay, experience, or camera loan is ever hosted or discounted, we state it upfront in both the video and written summary — and positive verdicts are never for sale.

How does your /25 hotel review scorecard work?

Every full hotel review on the channel is scored out of 5 across five equal categories (25 points total):

  • Rooms: Layout, sleep comfort, upkeep, privacy, and views.
  • Grounds & Vibe: Pools, beach/reef, architecture, and atmosphere.
  • Food & Drink: Breakfast quality, dining variety, and consistency.
  • Service: Warmth, speed, and genuine attention to detail.
  • Value: Whether the experience actually justifies the nightly rate or points cost.

Multiply the /25 score by 4 and you get the exact percentage used in StayOdds.

What cameras and microphones do you film with?

We travel light with compact, stabilized cameras that handle harsh tropical sun, dim villa interiors, and beach wind without needing a film crew — including the DJI Osmo Pocket 4P and Insta360 Luna Ultra.

Check out our hands-on Bali shootout on the Camera Reviews page, or browse our exact links and accessories on Deals & Gear.

How do you book luxury stays with points and save on travel?

We mix cash bookings with points redemptions — especially Marriott Bonvoy (including 5th Night Free on award stays) and flexible Amex Membership Rewards transfers — alongside travel eSIMs like Holafly so we stay connected right after landing.

Our active promo codes (including Holafly code TRIPREADY) and referral offers are kept up to date on the Deals & Gear page.

How do you film politely around other guests at luxury resorts?

Guest privacy comes first. We shoot pools, beaches, and restaurants early in the morning or during quiet windows, use small discreet pocket cameras rather than bulky rigs, avoid filming people sunbathing or dining up close, and always check property rules before flying a drone.

Why are some hotels only a Short tour without a /25 score?

We only hand out a full /25 personal scorecard when we’ve spent enough time on property to judge every category fairly (rooms, dining, service, grounds, and value). Quick visits, day passes, or 60-second villa tours are clearly marked as Short Tours rather than full scored reviews.

Do you only review ultra-luxury 5-star resorts?

Not at all. While luxury resorts and island retreats are a huge part of the channel, we also cover standout boutique stays, design-led city hotels, destination tours, flights, lounges, and smart travel gear — anywhere we think viewers get real value from an honest look.

Can hotels or travel brands work with The Uncharted Itinerary?

We’re open to selective media collaborations, press trips, and gear partnerships that genuinely fit our audience — provided we retain full editorial independence and clear disclosure. Reach out anytime via our Contact page.
