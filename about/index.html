Our Story

Sharp-eyed reviews for real-world travelers

Welcome to The Uncharted Itinerary. We believe travel is about far more than getting from A to B — it’s about the adventure in between, the places you explore, and whether the hotel you saved up for actually delivers once you check in.

Known for our deep dives into the automotive world on our sister channel, we bring that exact same eye for build quality, small details, and real value to travel — filming everything from remote island resorts and boutique villas to destination tours, flights, and pocket travel cameras.

Along the way, we kept hitting the same frustration every traveler faces: glossy five-star marketing photos rarely match reality, and it takes hours of bouncing between critic write-ups, YouTube tours, and guest reviews to figure out what a resort is genuinely like. That’s why we built StayOdds. (stayodds.com) — one clean place to line up our first-hand stays, published critics, official accolades, and real guest scores side-by-side.

What Guides Every Review & Score

Our three ground rules across every YouTube episode and StayOdds. profile.

01 • First-Hand Detail

Beyond the Brochure Photos

We walk through the real rooms, test breakfast and pools at normal guest hours, note construction noise or tired finishes, and call out whether the nightly rate or points cost feels genuinely fair.

02 • Zero Guesswork

Verified Sources, Never Invented Numbers

StayOdds. never makes up scores for awards or written reviews. Official accolades like MICHELIN Keys and Forbes Five-Star appear as direct links, while numeric scores blend transparently with live guest ratings.

03 • Independent Voice

Scores Cannot Be Bought

We pay for the vast majority of our stays and flights with our own cash or earned points. Any hosted stay, gifted gear, or affiliate link is clearly disclosed and never influences a score or ranking.
