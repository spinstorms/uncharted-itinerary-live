Privacy Policy & Disclosures

How The Uncharted Itinerary (uncharteditinerary.com) and StayOdds. (stayodds.com) handle data, cookies, advertising, and affiliate links.

Policy & Transparency Updated 2026
1. Overview

Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to The Uncharted Itinerary (uncharteditinerary.com) and StayOdds. (stayodds.com). We operate a public travel media hub and luxury hotel comparison directory that requires zero user account registration to browse. If you have any privacy questions, you can reach us at hello@uncharteditinerary.com.

2. Google AdSense & Advertising Cookies

Third-Party Display Advertising

We use Google AdSense (Publisher ID: pub-4618281487216833) to serve display advertisements across our site:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the DoubleClick advertising cookie) to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our visitors based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices or www.youronlinechoices.eu.
  • For more details on how Google uses data when you use our site, please review How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.
3. Affiliate Links & Editorial Independence

Affiliate & Partner Disclosure

The Uncharted Itinerary and StayOdds. participate in travel and gear affiliate programs, including hotel booking links (such as Booking.com via Travelpayouts), travel eSIMs, credit card referral links, and camera equipment links. When you click an outbound rate or product link and complete a booking or purchase, we may earn a small referral commission at no additional cost to you. Affiliate partnerships never influence which properties are included in StayOdds. or how any property’s StayOdds score, critic rating, or opinion gap is calculated.

4. Analytics & Browser Local Storage

How We Measure Traffic & Save Your Shortlist

  • Browser Local Storage: When you save hotels to your StayOdds. Shortlist or cache directory data for instant page loads, those preferences are stored locally in your own browser (localStorage) and are never transmitted to a central user profile database.
  • Website Analytics: We use privacy-conscious aggregate analytics (GoatCounter) and interaction heatmaps (Microsoft Clarity) to understand which hotel guides, filters, and tools visitors use most and to fix layout issues across mobile and desktop browsers.
  • Embedded Services: Pages may load embedded YouTube video players (only when you click to watch a review) and live Google Maps rating widgets governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
5. Voluntary Contact & Resort Requests

Information You Send Us

If you submit a message through our Contact form, send us a resort addition request, or email us directly at hello@uncharteditinerary.com, we use the name, email address, and message you provide solely to reply to your inquiry or review the requested hotel. We never sell or rent your email address or contact details to third parties.

6. Your Rights & Contact Details

Questions or Requests

You can clear browser cookies and local storage at any time through your web browser settings. If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, our affiliate disclosures, or data handling under GDPR/UK GDPR/CCPA, please contact us at hello@uncharteditinerary.com or via our Contact page.
